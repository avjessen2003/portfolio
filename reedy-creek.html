Environmental Science & Research

Post-Monitoring (2022-2024) of Groundwater Levels Along a Restored Stream

Reedy Creek, Charlotte, NC • UNC Charlotte OUR Program

This research study centers on stream restoration efforts at Reedy Creek, an urbanized watershed in northwest Charlotte. The project evaluates groundwater level and temperature shifts to determine hydrologic connectivity, floodplain recovery, and long-term ecological sustainability.

Key Project Contributions

  • Measured water levels and collected field samples to evaluate water quality parameters across 10 monitoring sites.
  • Deployed and maintained HOBO U20 and U20L pressure loggers for continuous water level and temperature logging.
  • Applied R statistical software for time-series data processing, noise removal, and barometric compensation.
  • Presented research findings on stream channel rehabilitation at the UNC Charlotte Undergraduate Research Conference (URC).

Tools & Methods Used

R Statistical Software HOBO Pressure Loggers Field Sampling Water Quality Analysis
