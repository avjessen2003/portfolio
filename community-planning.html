Deep Roots Farm Master Plan

Spatial Analysis & Community Land Use Framework

Conducted a comprehensive land use and spatial needs analysis for Deep Roots Farm in Monroe, NC, synthesizing site data to produce a functional, adaptive master plan layout.

Key Project Highlights

  • Synthesized spatial constraints, agricultural requirements, and community access to guide future site development.
  • Developed an adaptive master plan layout prioritizing functional circulation, land efficiency, and agricultural viability.
  • Translated stakeholder goals and environmental conditions into clear visual planning documentation.
