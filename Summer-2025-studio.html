Master's Urban Design Studio

Summer 2025 Urban Design Studio

UNC Charlotte • Master of Urban Design

Studio assignment presentation Open PDF ↗

This studio project explores adaptive reuse and urban revitalization by examining historic sites in Taipei, Taiwan and Uptown Charlotte, NC. The design balances historic preservation with creative spatial interventions, maintaining architectural character and cultural identity while transforming underutilized spaces into vibrant public destinations.

Key Project Highlights

  • Executed a cross-cultural site analysis comparing urban patterns in Taiwan and Uptown Charlotte to identify spatial opportunities and constraints for redevelopment.
  • Analyzed density, infrastructure, and public space frameworks in Taiwan, applying comparative findings to develop contextual planning solutions for Uptown Charlotte.
  • Constructed 3D spatial models and master plan layouts utilizing Rhino 3D.
  • Rendered detailed street sections and perspective views using Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign.

Tools & Methods Used
